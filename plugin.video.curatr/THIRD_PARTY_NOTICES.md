Curatr uses these Classic Solid icons:

`shield-halved`, `eye-slash`, `database`, `bell`, `arrows-rotate`,
`clock-rotate-left`, `gift`, `bookmark`, `pizza-slice`, `border-all`,
`cloud`, `folder-open`, `folder-plus`, `list-ul`, `film`, `tv`, `shuffle`,
`heart-pulse`, `fingerprint`, `file-lines`, and `layer-group`.

The original SVGs and their embedded attribution are retained in the source
package under `tools/icon_sources/fontawesome-7.0.1/`. Curatr converts them to
white PNG silhouettes, resizes and centres them, and adds transparent padding
or shared coloured backgrounds for menu, icon and fanart presentation.
Font Awesome does not endorse Curatr. Curatr's original code and artwork remain
under the terms in `LICENSE.txt`; this notice covers the listed third-party
icons and their adapted versions.

- Source: https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome/tree/6.7.2/svgs/solid

The right-hand action menus use these requested Classic Solid icons:
`caret-up`, `caret-down`, `xmark`, `trash`, `gear`, `filter`, `image`,
`circle-info`, `plus` and `bookmark`. The original SVGs and embedded attribution
are retained under `tools/icon_sources/fontawesome-6.7.2/`. Curatr centres and
converts them to padded white PNGs. The existing 7.0.1 `arrows-rotate` and
`folder-plus` icons are reused for Refresh/Sync and Add to Folder.
Keyword Matching uses the 6.7.2 `pen-to-square` and `check` icons for its
edit/done toggle beside Looking for, using the same retained-source and
padded-PNG conversion described above.
